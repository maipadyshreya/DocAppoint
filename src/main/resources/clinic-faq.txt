
CLINIC FAQ KNOWLEDGE BASE

Q: How do I book an appointment?
A: Log in, check available appointment slots, select a service and time, and confirm the booking.

Q: How do I cancel an appointment?
A: Log in, view your appointments, select the appointment you want to cancel, and confirm cancellation.

Q: Can I view my appointments?
A: Yes. Logged-in patients can view their appointment history.

Q: Can I book an appointment without logging in?
A: No. Patients must be logged in to book appointments.

Q: Can I book an appointment for another patient?
A: No. Appointments are associated with the logged-in patient's account.

Q: How do appointment reminders work?
A: The system checks for scheduled appointments within the next 24 hours and generates personalized reminders.

Q: Can I cancel another patient's appointment?
A: No. Patients can cancel only appointments associated with their own account.

Q: What happens after I cancel an appointment?
A: The appointment is marked as cancelled. If the appointment slot is in the future, it becomes available for booking again.
